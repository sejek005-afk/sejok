What Eshimiz is

Eshimiz is a bookkeeping app for construction contractors: projects, daily progress, crew and attendance, installers, contractors, invoices, progress statements, cheques and notes. This page explains what data is kept and what we do with it. The Persian version is the reference text.

What is stored

  • Your account: name, mobile number (used as your username) and password. The password is stored only as a one-way hash; nobody, including the server admin, can read it.
  • Data you enter: projects, amounts, names and phone numbers of your crew, installers, contractors and customers, invoices, cheques, notes, your logo and stamp.
  • Support messages: suggestions and tickets you send from “Support & feedback”, with your name, number, app version and phone model, are kept on the server and forwarded to our support email so we can reply.
  • Technical data: your phone model, to list devices signed in to your account, and your IP address, to block repeated failed sign-ins.

Eshimiz has no ads. It does not read your location, contacts, photos or messages, and contains no third-party tracking or analytics.

Where it is kept and who can see it

  • Data is kept on your phone and on the Eshimiz server (eshim.ir) so it is available on your other phones. The connection to the server is always encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each account’s data is visible only to its owner and to people the owner has shared it with by entering their mobile number. The owner can remove that access at any time.
  • We do not sell, rent or hand over your data, except when required by a competent legal authority.

App permissions

  • Internet: to sign in and sync with the server.
  • Notifications, alarms and vibration: for note and cheque reminders at times you set.
  • Run at startup: to restore reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Files: only when you save or choose a PDF or backup file yourself.
  • SMS and calls: the app never sends messages itself; a button only opens your phone’s messaging or dialer app with the number filled in.

Deleting data and your account

Anything you delete in the app is also deleted from the server. To delete your account completely, go to Settings → Delete account. Your account and all data in “My account” are permanently removed from the server; this cannot be undone. You can save a backup file to your phone from Settings first.

Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of the amounts and information you enter. The app’s calculations help you keep your books; they are not financial or legal advice.
  • Keep your password to yourself. To give others access, use account sharing.
  • Although the server is backed up nightly, we recommend saving a backup file on your phone from time to time.
