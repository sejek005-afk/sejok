From the job site to your phone

Every app follows the same path Eshimiz did.

  1. We look at the real work

    First we learn how the books are kept today, on paper or in someone’s head.

  2. We take version one on site

    A few days in the hands of the people who will use it, fixing whatever gets in their way.

  3. We release and support it

    Store release, server and nightly backups, and the updates that follow.
